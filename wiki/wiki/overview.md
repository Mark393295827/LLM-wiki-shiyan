## Central Theme

The wiki currently focuses on **knowledge management methodologies**, specifically the contrast between traditional retrieval approaches (like [[RAG]]) and the emerging [[LLM-Wiki Pattern]] of persistent, compiled knowledge wikis.

### Key Insight
> The fundamental shift is from **retrieval** (re-find knowledge per query) to **compilation** (build once, maintain incrementally). The LLM handles the maintenance work that makes humans abandon wikis.
