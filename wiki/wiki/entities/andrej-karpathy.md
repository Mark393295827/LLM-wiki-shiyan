# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator known for foundational work in deep learning and computer vision.

## Key Contributions

- Former Director of AI at Tesla (Autopilot)
- Founding member of OpenAI
- Creator of popular ML courses (CS231n at Stanford)
- Author of the [[LLM-Wiki Pattern|llm-wiki]] concept for personal knowledge management

## Ideas

### LLM-Wiki Pattern (2026)

Proposed a pattern where LLMs maintain a **persistent, compounding knowledge wiki** rather than using traditional [[RAG]] approaches. Key insight: compile knowledge once and keep it current, rather than re-derive on every query.

> "Obsidian is the IDE; the LLM is the programmer; the wiki is the codebase."
