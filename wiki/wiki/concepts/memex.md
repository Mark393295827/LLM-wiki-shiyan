# Memex

A hypothetical device envisioned by [[Vannevar Bush]] in 1945 ("As We May Think") — a mechanized desk with microfilm storage that would let a person store all their books, records, and communications, then retrieve and link them through associative "trails."

## Key Ideas

- Personal, curated knowledge store
- Associative trails between documents (proto-hyperlinks)
- The **connections** between documents as valuable as the documents themselves
- Private, actively curated

## Connection to [[LLM-Wiki Pattern]]

Bush's vision was closer to the llm-wiki pattern than to what the web became:
- Private, actively curated ✓
- Associative connections ✓
- The unsolved problem: **who does the maintenance?**

> The LLM handles the part Bush couldn't solve — the ongoing maintenance and cross-referencing.
